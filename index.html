PLAN WITH PEACE OF MIND

A little prep goes a long way.

Your itinerary begins at Batangas Port. Confirm the final meeting point, current ferry arrangements, and schedule with Lakventure before departure.

Best time to visit

Many visitors prefer the drier months for beach and boat activities, but conditions vary. Check the local forecast and sea advisories before travel.

Getting there

The listed itinerary starts at Batangas Port. Confirm ferry operator, departure terminal, ticket requirements, meeting point, and travel time before leaving.

Budget planning

Set aside money for meals not included, personal purchases, tips if desired, and unexpected transport costs. Confirm all fees before paying a deposit.

Safety & accessibility

Ask about stairs, uneven paths, swimming ability requirements, life jackets, medical access, and accessibility needs. Do not enter caves or swim when local staff advise against it.

Local etiquette

Respect residents and local rules, ask permission before photographing people, dress appropriately for the setting, and follow guide instructions.

Emergency readiness

Save the local emergency contact numbers before the trip, keep your phone charged, share your itinerary with a trusted person, and follow official local advisories.

TRANSPARENT TRIP BUDGETING

Where the price figures come from.

Rates below are reference points from published sources, not binding quotes. Confirm current fares, fees, boat capacity, activity inclusions, and booking terms with the relevant operator before travel.

01

Ferry fare reference

Galera Guide lists an adult tourist fare of ₱750 one way for Island Water Shipping Lines, last updated September 27, 2026. The source warns that fares can change; verify directly with the shipping line.

View ferry fare source ↗
02

Port and environmental fees

The same fare guide lists a ₱90 Batangas terminal fee, ₱30 Balatero terminal fee, and ₱120 environmental user fee (EUF). PIA independently reports the ₱120 EUF under Municipal Ordinance No. 2025-030.

View fee breakdown ↗·Read Philippine Information Agency report ↗
03

Island-hopping boat reference

The Pinay Solo Backpacker reports a typical ₱1,800 shared boat rate for up to six people (about ₱300 each when split by six). This is a travel-guide reference, not an official municipal tariff or guaranteed operator quote.

View island-hopping cost reference ↗
04

Local tour price varies

Published local tour pages show that inclusions and prices vary by provider. One current 2026 package page lists optional guided land and island tours but asks visitors to request a quote for package prices. This is why the sample eco-tour is marked quote-required.

View 2026 package information ↗·View tour options ↗
How the baseline is calculated: ₱750 × 2 ferry legs + ₱90 Batangas terminal fee + ₱30 Balatero terminal fee + ₱120 EUF = ₱1,740 per adult before local transfers, meals, accommodation, and optional activities. For the island-hopping baseline, add ₱1,800 ÷ 6 = ₱300 per person, giving ₱2,040+. These calculations use published third-party references and must be rechecked before booking.
